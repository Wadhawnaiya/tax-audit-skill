# Form 26 transition — read this before starting ANY engagement

Last verified: 20-Nov-2026 (Income Tax Department Form 26 FAQs, Income-tax Rules 2026 notified 20-Mar-2026).

## Which form applies?

| Previous year / tax year | Law | Audit section | Form |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) and earlier | Income-tax Act, 1961 | Section 44AB, Rule 6G | **Form 3CA/3CB + 3CD** ← this skill |
| Tax Year 2026-27 (1-Apr-2026 to 31-Mar-2027) onward | Income-tax Act, 2025 | Section 63, Rule 47 of Income-tax Rules 2026 | **Form No. 26** |

**This skill drafts Form 3CA/3CB-3CD for AY 2026-27 only.** If the user's client documents are for tax year
2026-27 or later (period starting 1-Apr-2026 or later), STOP and tell the user:
- the engagement falls under Form No. 26, which this version of the skill does not yet draft;
- you can still help with document review, reconciliations and a clause-wise issue list, but the clause numbers,
  section references and schema in `references/` do not apply.

Do not "translate" 3CD answers into Form 26 by guessing section numbers. All references in Form 26 are to the
2025 Act and the 2026 Rules, never to the 1961 Act.

## What is known about Form 26 (for planning the next rebuild)

- **Part A** – particulars of the assessee. **Part B** – statement of particulars (replaces 3CD; applies in all
  cases). **Part C** – audit report where accounts are audited under another law (replaces 3CA). **Part D** –
  audit report where accounts are not audited under another law (replaces 3CB).
- Due date: one month before the return due date under section 263(1) — e.g. 30 September where the return is
  due 31 October.
- Section 63 thresholds mirror the old 44AB thresholds: business turnover above Rs. 1 crore (Rs. 10 crore if cash
  receipts and cash payments each stay within 5%); profession gross receipts above Rs. 50 lakh; presumptive
  opt-out cases.
- Design changes reported in the departmental FAQs: mandatory Yes/No answer for every clause with schedules only
  for "Yes"; depreciation split by usage period (more than / up to 180 days); quantitative details only for
  trading or manufacturing assessees; audit observations categorised as test-check / management representation /
  unable to verify, with profit impact stated; new disclosures on accounting software and server/backup location;
  TDS/TCS reporting includes transactions not reported in TDS/TCS returns.

The workpaper approach in this skill (status flags, source citations, reconciliations, impact column, CA review
register) carries over directly. The clause guides, thresholds and canonical clause list do not.
